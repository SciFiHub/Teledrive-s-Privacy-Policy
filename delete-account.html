Account & Data Deletion

Request deletion of your Teledrive account and associated data.

How to request deletion

To request deletion of your Teledrive account and associated cloud metadata, email our support team using the link below.

Email an account deletion request

Alternatively, send an email to teledriveapp@gmail.com with the subject Teledrive Account Deletion Request.

  1. Tell us that you want to delete your Teledrive account and associated data.
  2. Include your Telegram username if you have one. Do not include your Telegram password, login code, session token, or private files.
  3. We may ask you to verify account ownership through a secure process before fulfilling the request.
  4. We will confirm the request and explain its status or any applicable retention exception.

You do not need to install or sign in to Teledrive to open this webpage or send a deletion request.

What the deletion request covers

Following verification, a request to delete your Teledrive account is intended to remove account-linked data maintained by Epsilon lab inc for Teledrive, including, where applicable:

  • Teledrive account identity and authentication/session records.
  • Cloud-synchronized file and folder metadata, including names, hierarchy, starred status, and ordering.
  • Telegram message references, synchronization records, and related account associations.

Deletion from active systems may not immediately remove data from encrypted backups or records that must be retained for legitimate legal or security purposes. We will explain applicable exceptions in our response.

What is not deleted

Deleting a Teledrive account does not delete your Telegram account. Telegram-hosted messages, files, channels, and Saved Messages are managed by Telegram and your Telegram account. Teledrive's deletion process does not itself erase that content from Telegram.

Data stored only on your device, such as local caches, may need to be cleared through the app or Android settings. If your request concerns only selected files or metadata rather than the whole Teledrive account, explain that in your email.

Processing and retention

We aim to process verified deletion requests without undue delay and within applicable legal deadlines. The precise time required can depend on identity verification, technical recovery safeguards, and legal requirements. Some limited records may be retained where legally necessary, and backup copies may remain until scheduled expiration.

For more information about processing, retention and your rights, see our Privacy Policy.
