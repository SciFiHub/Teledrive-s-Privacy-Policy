Privacy Policy

How we handle Telegram-connected file metadata, authentication, local storage, service providers, and your rights.

Effective date: 14 November 2026 · Last updated: 14 November 2026

1. Who We Are

Teledrive, developed by Epsilon lab inc (“we”, “us”, “our”) is an independent Android file-management application that organizes content associated with a user's Telegram account. This policy covers our app, authentication and metadata-sync services, and support communications. It does not replace Telegram's own policy.

Operator / data controller: Epsilon lab inc. Business address and country: Uttar Pradesh, India . Privacy email: teledriveapp@gmail.com. Data-protection or grievance contact: Epsilon lab inc — epsilonlabinc@gmail.com.

Teledrive is not affiliated with or endorsed by Telegram unless explicitly stated otherwise.

2. Summary

  • Telegram stores the underlying media and messages through your Telegram account. Our intended backend architecture stores organizational metadata, not full copies of your Telegram media.
  • We use Telegram account identifiers and secure authentication information to associate metadata with the correct account.
  • We store account-scoped folder/file metadata, Telegram message references, sync history, revisions and preferences in a backend database and local SQLite storage.
  • Thumbnails and previews may be processed and temporarily cached on your device.
  • Technical/security logs may be generated to operate and protect the service.
  • Future ads, analytics or subscriptions require further disclosures and controls before activation; this document does not say they are currently active.

4. Sources and Purposes

We receive data directly from you, your authorized Telegram session, app interactions, local storage, our authentication and synchronization services, and operational service providers. We process it to authenticate users, show and organize files, sync metadata, enable requested transfers, provide support, prevent abuse, diagnose failures and comply with legal obligations.

We do not intentionally scan unrelated Telegram chats for advertising. The exact channels, Saved Messages and messages available to the app depend on the features you use and permissions granted through Telegram.

6. Telegram and Media

Teledrive uses TDLib and Telegram services to interact with your account and content. Under the intended architecture, full media transfers directly between your device and Telegram rather than being stored on our metadata backend. Our backend may store message identifiers and organizational information that can reveal file names and activity.

Telegram independently handles its services and data under Telegram's Privacy Policy. We cannot guarantee that Telegram messages or media will remain available if they are deleted, access changes, or Telegram changes its services.

7. Device Storage and Permissions

The app may use account-scoped SQLite databases, Android secure credential storage, temporary thumbnails and previews, transfer queues, and offline synchronization records. Temporary files may remain until cleanup or operating-system eviction. We do not promise that media bytes never touch local storage during previews or downloads.

Permissions may include internet/network, notifications, foreground services and access to files/media you choose. We request permissions only as necessary for implemented functions. The permissions available depend on the installed app version and Android settings; users can review them in Android Settings.

9. Retention and Deletion

We retain metadata while needed for your account and the service, and for limited backup, fraud prevention, disputes and legal obligations. Logging out does not automatically delete cloud metadata. Deleting a Teledrive folder or entry may not delete its underlying Telegram message. Account deletion and data deletion must be clearly distinguished.

Retention depends on the type of data and why it is needed. Account metadata generally remains while the account uses cloud synchronization; session information remains while needed for authentication and security; operational logs, deleted-record history, support correspondence and backups are kept only as long as reasonably necessary for their respective purposes, security, recovery or legal requirements. Backup copies may remain until their scheduled expiry. For information about a particular category or deletion request, email us. We do not promise immediate erasure from every backup.

Request deletion or access via teledriveapp@gmail.com or teledriveapp@gmail.com. If you request deletion, we will explain the scope, verify your account where appropriate, and process the request subject to applicable law. Removing Teledrive metadata does not by itself delete your Telegram account or content stored by Telegram.
